# Contributing to Awesome CRA

Thanks for helping build a practical, security-focused CRA resource.

## Principles

- **Real and reachable only.** Every link must resolve and point to a resource a practitioner can actually use. No dead links, no placeholder pages.
- **Engineering over commentary.** Prefer primary sources, standards, and tooling over opinion pieces. Legal commentary is included only when it clarifies scope or obligations.
- **No filler.** One strong entry beats five weak ones. Marketing pages and vendor landing pages without substance are rejected.
- **Neutral descriptions.** One line, factual, no hype.

## How to add an entry

1. Fork the repo.
2. Add your entry under the most relevant section, alphabetically where sections are unordered.
3. Format: `` - [Name](https://url) - short factual description ending with a period.``
4. Run the link check locally if possible.
5. Open a pull request describing what you added and why it belongs.

## What gets rejected

- Broken or redirect-farm links.
- Duplicate resources already covered.
- Generic "top 10 compliance tips" content with no primary-source or tooling value.
- Anything paywalled without a usable free portion.

## Tooling contributions

For changes to tools in `tools/`, include a short description of the observable behavior change and, where relevant, sample input/output. Keep functions small and testable.
